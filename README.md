# Hi, I’m Wenwen (Celine) 👋

I’m an empirical researcher with a background in development economics and public policy. My current work focuses on **human judgment, learning, and accountability in AI-assisted work**.

What draws me to this research is a broader interest in **human flourishing**. I care about people having room to learn, reflect, connect with others, and decide what matters to them. As AI becomes part of everyday work, I want to understand when it expands those opportunities. When AI helps someone make a decision today, what do they learn that they can bring to the next one?

## Current Research Focus

When someone is responsible for approving an AI recommendation, what helps them check it and act on what they find?

- **Human review and interface design.** I’m designing a study on whether placing source evidence beside an AI recommendation helps reviewers catch mistakes or makes the recommendation more convincing.
- **Organizational and institutional conditions.** I’m interested in how workplace incentives, review practices, and governance rules shape people’s ability to question recommendations, correct errors, and learn from the outcome.
